Why schools move off registers and Excel files

Most schools do not have a software problem — they have a record problem. Fee defaulters are found weeks late, attendance registers cannot be searched, result compilation eats a full week every term, and parents call the office for information a screen could have shown them. A school management system turns those scattered registers into one searchable record that the principal, the accounts office and the class teacher all read from.

Features included in this system

Every module below is built to match how your business already works.

Admissions & student records

Admission forms, student profiles, documents, guardian details, class and section assignment, promotion and leaving certificates.

Attendance system

Daily class-wise attendance for students and staff, monthly percentages, leave records and absentee alerts to parents.

Fee management & challans

Generate monthly challans in bulk, apply discounts and concessions, record payments, and see defaulter lists instantly.

Exams, marks & report cards

Exam scheduling, datesheets, marks entry, grading rules, position calculation and printable report cards.

Timetable & classes

Class, section and subject setup, teacher allocation and timetable generation without clashes.

Staff payroll & salaries

Staff records, salary structures, deductions, advances and monthly salary sheets.

Parent & student portal

Parents log in to check attendance, fee status, results and announcements — or receive them by SMS and WhatsApp.

Management reports

Enrolment trends, collection vs outstanding, class-wise performance and expense summaries for the management.

Frequently asked questions

What business owners ask us before starting.

What modules are included in the school management system?

Admissions and student records, attendance, fee management and challans, exams and report cards, timetable, staff payroll, parent and student portal, and management reports. Library, transport, hostel and inventory modules can be added.

Can it send fee reminders to parents?

Yes. The system can send fee, attendance and result notifications by SMS or WhatsApp, and parents can also check the same information in the portal.

Can it handle multiple branches or campuses?

Yes. Each campus can be managed separately with its own classes, staff and fee structure, while management sees a combined view.

Can we transfer our existing student and fee data?

Yes. Student records, class allocations, fee structures and outstanding balances can usually be imported from Excel so you do not start from zero.

How long does a school management system take to build?

A standard setup typically takes six to ten weeks depending on modules. We agree the timeline in writing and show working progress along the way.
