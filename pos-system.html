What our POS software does for your business

Most shops lose money in the gaps: stock that was never recorded, discounts nobody approved, and a day's sales that only gets counted at night. A proper POS system closes those gaps. Every sale updates inventory instantly, every invoice is traceable, and the profit figure at the end of the day is calculated from real purchase cost, not guesswork.

Features included in this system

Every module below is built to match how your business already works.

Fast barcode billing

Scan or search, apply discounts, split payments and print or share the invoice. The counter keeps moving even at peak hours.

Live inventory control

Stock moves the moment a sale or purchase is recorded. Low-stock alerts tell you what to reorder before you run out.

Invoices & returns

Professional printed or PDF invoices, sales returns, exchanges and credit notes — all linked to the original bill.

Customer & supplier ledgers

Track udhaar, payments received, supplier balances and payment history without a separate register.

Sales & profit reports

Daily, weekly, monthly or any custom date range. See top products, slow movers, expenses and net profit.

Staff roles & permissions

Give cashiers what they need and keep pricing, discounts and reports restricted to owners and managers.

Multi-branch ready

Run several outlets and see each one separately or combined in a single owner dashboard.

Works offline or on cloud

Keep billing running when internet drops, then sync. Or go fully cloud so you can check sales from your phone.

Frequently asked questions

What business owners ask us before starting.

How much does a POS system cost in Pakistan?

Price depends on scope. A single-counter POS with billing, inventory and reports is the entry point; multi-branch systems with user roles, cloud sync and hardware integration cost more. You get a fixed written quote before development starts.

Does the POS work with barcode scanners and thermal printers?

Yes. The system works with standard USB barcode scanners, thermal receipt printers, cash drawers and label printers. We test your exact hardware during setup.

Can I use it on mobile as well as a computer?

Yes. We can build it as a web-based system that opens on any computer, tablet or phone browser, so owners can check sales remotely while staff bill from the counter.

Can you import my existing product list?

Yes. Products, prices, opening stock, customers and suppliers can be imported from Excel or CSV so you start with real data.

Is training included?

Yes. We train your staff on the system, hand over documentation and stay available for support after launch.
