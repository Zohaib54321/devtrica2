Restaurant Management System

Restaurant Management System for Dine-In, Takeaway & Delivery

Take the order, fire it to the kitchen, split the bill and close the day with a report you can trust — from one screen your staff can learn in an afternoon.

Get a Free Quote Ask on WhatsApp

Built for how a busy kitchen actually runs

Restaurants do not lose money on the menu price. They lose it on orders that never reached the kitchen, wastage nobody logged, and staff meals counted as sales. A restaurant POS ties the floor, the kitchen and the store together so every order is recorded once and every item that leaves the store is accounted for.

Features included in this system

Every module below is built to match how your business already works.

Table & floor management

See which tables are open, occupied or billed. Merge and transfer tables, and handle multiple floors or sections.

KOT to the kitchen

Orders print or display in the kitchen instantly, with modifiers, special instructions and preparation status.

Dine-in, takeaway & delivery

Handle all three order types with rider assignment, delivery charges and customer addresses saved for repeat orders.

Menu & deal control

Categories, variations, add-ons, deals and time-based pricing — update your menu without calling a developer.

Split bills & payments

Split by item or person, apply discounts, handle cash, card and online payments, and print or share receipts.

Recipe-level inventory

Deduct ingredients as dishes are sold, track wastage and see which items are eating your margin.

Shift & staff control

Cashier shifts, opening and closing cash, void and discount approvals, and per-user activity logs.

Daily closing reports

Sales by item, category, order type and hour, plus expenses and net profit for any date range.

Frequently asked questions

What business owners ask us before starting.

Does the restaurant POS support kitchen display screens?

Yes. Orders can print as KOT slips on a kitchen printer, display on a kitchen screen, or both.

Can it handle multiple branches?

Yes. Each branch runs its own counter while owners see combined sales, item performance and stock across all locations.

Can we integrate delivery orders?

Yes. Delivery orders can be entered with rider assignment and delivery charges, and we can connect to third-party platforms where an API is available.

Will it work if the internet goes down?

Yes. The system can run locally so billing continues offline and syncs once the connection returns.

Can we change the menu ourselves?

Yes. Menu items, categories, deals and prices are managed from an admin screen by your own staff.
