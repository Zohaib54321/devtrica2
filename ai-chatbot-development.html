From answering questions to taking action

Most businesses lose enquiries to response time, not to price. Someone asks about availability at midnight, gets no reply, and orders elsewhere by morning. A chatbot closes that gap by answering instantly from your own information. An AI agent goes further: it checks stock, creates the order, logs the lead and follows up — steps that currently sit on someone's to-do list.

Frequently asked questions

What business owners ask us before starting.

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent takes action — checking inventory, creating an order, logging a lead or following up — without a person doing it manually.

Can the chatbot answer in Urdu?

Yes. The chatbot can reply in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu depending on how the customer writes.

Can it be added to WhatsApp?

Yes. We can deploy on your website, on WhatsApp through the official Business API, or both with shared conversation history.

How does it know about my business?

We train it on your own material: product lists, prices, services, policies and past customer questions. It answers from that, not from guesswork.

What happens when the bot cannot answer?

It hands the conversation to your team with the full history, so the customer never has to repeat themselves.
