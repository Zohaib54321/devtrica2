Frequently Asked Questions

The questions business owners ask us most often before starting a project.

How much does a POS system cost in Pakistan?

The cost depends on what your business actually needs. A single-counter retail POS with billing, inventory and reports costs far less than a multi-branch system with user roles, barcode hardware and cloud sync. Send us your requirements on WhatsApp and you get a fixed written quote before any work begins — no hourly surprises.

What features are included in your school management system?

Our school management software includes student admissions and records, staff records, daily attendance, fee challan generation and collection tracking, exam scheduling, marks entry, automatic report cards, class timetables, salary management, SMS or WhatsApp notifications, and a login portal for parents and students. Extra modules such as library, transport and hostel can be added.

Can the POS software work without internet?

Yes. We can build the system to run offline on your local computer so billing never stops when the connection drops, and sync to the cloud when internet returns. If you need branch-to-branch reporting, the cloud version keeps every location in one dashboard.

How long does it take to build a custom system?

A business website usually takes one to two weeks. A standard POS or pharmacy system takes around three to five weeks. A full school management system or a custom multi-module platform typically takes six to ten weeks. We agree the timeline in writing at the start and show you working progress along the way.

Do you provide training and support after delivery?

Yes. We train your staff on the system, hand over documentation, and stay available for fixes and improvements after launch. Most clients keep us on for ongoing changes as their business grows.

Can you move my existing data from Excel or another software?

In most cases yes. Products, customers, suppliers, opening stock, student records and fee history can usually be imported from Excel, CSV or a database export so you start with your real data instead of an empty system.

Do you work with businesses outside Lahore?

Yes. We work with clients across Pakistan and internationally. Requirements, demos, training and support are all handled online over WhatsApp, calls and screen sharing, so your location is not a limitation.

What is an AI agent and how is it different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent takes action — it can check your inventory, create an order, log a lead in your system, follow up with a customer or move information between tools without a person doing it manually. Many businesses start with a chatbot and grow into agents once they see what gets automated.
