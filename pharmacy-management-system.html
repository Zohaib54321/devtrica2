Pharmacy Management System

Pharmacy Management System With Real Batch & Expiry Control

Know exactly which batch is on the shelf, what expires next month, and what each sale actually earned — instead of finding out when the stock is already dead.

Expiry is the real cost in a pharmacy

A medical store can be busy all month and still lose money to expired stock and untracked returns. Pharmacy software that works at batch level tells you which packs expire first, which supplier they came from, and which items sit for months without selling — early enough to return, discount or stop reordering them.

Features included in this system

Every module below is built to match how your business already works.

Medicine master & batches

Generic and brand names, formula, company, rack location, pack size and per-batch purchase and sale rates.

Expiry & near-expiry alerts

See what expires this month, next month or in any range, and act on it before it becomes a loss.

Purchases & supplier returns

Record purchase invoices batch-wise, manage supplier ledgers, and process returns and credit notes cleanly.

Fast counter billing

Search by brand or generic name, pick the right batch automatically, apply discounts and print the invoice.

Low stock alerts

Minimum stock levels per item with a reorder list you can hand straight to your supplier.

Sales returns & exchanges

Link returns to the original invoice so stock and accounts stay correct.

User roles & audit trail

Restrict rate changes, discounts and deletions, and keep a log of who did what.

Profit & movement reports

Daily sales, item-wise profit, fast and slow movers, supplier-wise purchases and expense tracking.

Frequently asked questions

What business owners ask us before starting.

Does the pharmacy software track medicines by batch and expiry?

Yes. Every purchase is recorded batch-wise with its own expiry date, purchase rate and sale rate, and billing picks the correct batch automatically.

Can it alert me before medicines expire?

Yes. You can see near-expiry stock for any date range and get alerts early enough to return or clear it.

Does it handle supplier ledgers and returns?

Yes. Purchase invoices, payments, supplier balances, purchase returns and credit notes are all handled inside the system.

Can I search medicines by generic name?

Yes. Items can be searched by brand, generic, formula or company, which also helps staff suggest available alternatives.

Can it be used for a distribution business?

Yes. We can extend it with sales orders, delivery challans, area-wise customers and salesman-wise reporting.
